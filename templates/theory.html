UNIT 05 / CAP + HDFS

Design for a break.

During a partition, the AP simulation returns a stale replica value; the CP simulation rejects the read until a quorum is reachable. Reconciliation makes both replicas converge.

The HDFS teaching model assigns three replicas to each block. Losing one node leaves two copies. This models placement, not a running HDFS service.
